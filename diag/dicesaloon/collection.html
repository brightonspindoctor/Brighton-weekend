REGULAR EVENTS

Friday Night Age of Sigmar? Tuesday night X-Wing? Kids Club Adventures? We host several recurring events throughout the week. If you don't see what you're looking for - be sure to ask.

SPECIAL EVENTS

Whether it be a Bring & Buy for all your board game needs or a one-off Blood on the Clocktower morning or even a 60 person war gaming tournament, we host many special events over the weekends.

TCG EVENTS

Our dedicated TCG team over at Dice Saloon Singles host many events around all your favourite trading card games. From Magic the Gathering to Star Wars Unlimited to Pokemon, there's something for everyone. Visit their website for their events.

BOOKING FOR A PARTY?

Booking for a birthday? Interested in running an event with us? Got a group of lots of people wanting to play games for an afternoon? Reach out and contact us using the Chat button, or by our contact form. We'd love to hear from you.

How to Book an Event Ticket

  • On the calendar find the day of the event you'd like a ticket for and click on it.
  • Scroll down to see the event and its description.
  • Select how many tickets you'd like to book.
  • Then click Check Out!
  • You can see your tickets through your orders on the account page or from the "View Your Order" button on the confirmation email.
  • Have your QR code available when you arrive for your event and you're good to roll!
