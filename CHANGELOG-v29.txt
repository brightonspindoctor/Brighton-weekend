Brighton Weekend v29 — Happenings, PWA icon and mobile layout fix

FIXES
1. Happenings now shows a complete roster of everyone in every group you belong to, not just people who have an event activity.
2. Interested/Bought activity still includes your own activity.
3. Event Interested/Bought counts are de-duplicated by user, so someone who shares multiple groups cannot inflate the count; your Bought action no longer appears as 2.
4. Added extra bottom safe-area/content padding so the fixed bottom navigation cannot cover the final event card.
5. Strengthened PWA/Home Screen icon metadata with a versioned Apple touch icon, explicit sizes, manifest id, and maskable icon entries; service-worker cache bumped to v29.
6. iOS install instructions now explicitly direct users through Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
