About Brighton Weekend

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Discover. Plan. Go together.

Brighton Weekend is a web app for discovering events in Brighton and Hove and making plans with friends and groups.

Discover events

Browse events by weekend, venue and category, including music, comedy, theatre and nightlife.

Plan together

Create or join groups and share event interest with people in the same group. Happenings brings together relevant activity from people who share a group with you.

Community events

Members can add community events to help their groups coordinate plans.

Google Sign-In

Brighton Weekend uses Google Sign-In to create and authenticate accounts. We use basic Google account information, such as your name and email address, to provide account and group functionality. See our Privacy Policy for details.
