BRIGHTON WEEKEND

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

In brief: we collect only what's needed to run Brighton Weekend: your name, email address, groups and the events you respond to. People in your groups can see your name, avatar and responses. We don't sell your data, show adverts or use tracking cookies. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

1. Who we are

Brighton Weekend (brightonweekend.co.uk) is a web app for discovering events in Brighton and Hove and making plans with friends. It is run by John Sadler, based in Brighton, UK, who is the data controller responsible for your personal information under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

For any privacy question or request, email privacy@brightonweekend.co.uk.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a user ID. If you sign in with Google, Google also shares your name and, where available, profile picture. We only use your name and email address.
  • What you add: your display name, chosen avatar, the groups you create or join, your responses to events (Interested, Going or Not for me) and any community events you add.
  • Sign-in emails: if you sign in by email, we use your address to send you a one-time sign-in link.
  • Usage record: your user ID, display name and when you last used the app, so we can see how many people use Brighton Weekend.
  • Security records: if a group PIN is entered incorrectly, we briefly record your user ID and the time, to stop PINs being guessed.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page.

4. Who can see your information

  • People in your groups can see your display name, avatar, the events you respond to and your group membership. You choose which groups to join, and you can leave a group at any time.
  • Anyone using Brighton Weekend can see community events you add. People who are signed in can also see your display name as the person who added them. You can remove an event you added at any time.
  • Service providers (below) process data on our behalf to run the app.

6. International transfers

Your account and app data are stored by Supabase in London, UK. Some of our other providers (such as Google, Resend, GitHub and jsDelivr) are based in, or process data in, the United States or other countries outside the UK. Where that happens, transfers are protected by recognised safeguards, such as the UK–US data bridge, or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to standard contractual clauses, as provided in each provider's data processing terms.

7. Storage on your device

The app stores a few items in your browser so that it works: your sign-in session, your venue, day and avatar choices, your display name, and a record of what you'd already seen (for the "since your last visit" summary and the new-activity dot). They are used only to provide the app on that device. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes them.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account and app data (profile, groups, responses, community events, usage record): for as long as you have an account.
  • When you delete your account in Settings, it is deleted straight away. If you ask by email instead, we delete it within 30 days. Our providers may keep backups for a short further period before they are overwritten.
  • Failed PIN attempt records: within 24 hours.
  • Groups are removed once their last member leaves or deletes their account.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access a copy of your personal information;
  • correct it (you can change your name in Settings);
  • delete it (use Delete my account in Settings, or email us);
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including anything based on legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

Email privacy@brightonweekend.co.uk to use any of these rights. We'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, although we'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

10. Age

Brighton Weekend is for people aged 18 or over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. For example, only signed-in users can use the app's data, each person can only change their own information, and group activity is shared only with members of that group. No internet service can guarantee complete security, and if a breach affected you in a way that put you at risk, we would tell you.

12. Changes

We'll update this policy if the app or how we use data changes, and change the date at the top. If a change significantly affects you, we'll let you know in the app.
